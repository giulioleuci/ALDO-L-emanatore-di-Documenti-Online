# Icon Design Prompt for ALDO

## Project Context

**ALDO** = *Aldo, L'emanatore di Documenti Online*

An ERP system for Italian schools built on Google Workspace. Automates administrative and didactic workflows: document generation at scale, personalized email notifications, data import, and structured Drive archiving. Serves entire school community (teachers, students, staff, families).

---

## Core Functions to Represent

1. **Document Emanation** — ALDO creates and distributes documents automatically
2. **Communication Hub** — Connects all school actors (teachers, students, admin, families)
3. **Data-Driven Automation** — Intelligent workflows from structured data
4. **School Administration** — Efficiency, order, reliability
5. **Accessibility** — Should signal "easy to use" despite complexity

---

## Design Requirements

### Symbolism & Metaphor

- **Primary metaphor: "Emanation"** — visual suggestion that documents/information flow *outward* from a central source (Aldo as creator/notifier)
- **Secondary metaphor: "Community/Connection"** — subtle representation of interconnected school actors
- **Tertiary metaphor: "Clarity/Structure"** — sense of order, organization (data-driven, not chaotic)

Avoid:
- Clichéd school symbols (apple, pencil, graduation cap alone)
- Overly literal document/envelope imagery
- Generic corporate/business vibes

---

### Visual Style

- **Aesthetic:** Modern, clean, geometric; slightly playful without being childish
- **Complexity:** Scalable from 512px favicon down to 24px UI icon (works at all sizes)
- **Palette:**
  - Primary: One anchor color signaling "education + trust" (consider deep blue, deep green, or warm orange)
  - Secondary: One accent for "movement/energy" (lighter complement or vibrant accent)
  - Monochrome fallback: Must remain legible and elegant in grayscale
- **Stroke/Weight:** Balanced; crisp lines, no anti-aliasing artifacts at small scale

---

### Core Visual Elements (Optional Suggestions)

Choose imagery that fuses:

1. **Emanation concept:**
   - Radiating lines or ripples outward from center (like a stone in water, or document spreading)
   - Concentric circles suggesting broadcast/diffusion
   - Starburst or rays emerging from a focal point

2. **Document/Data:**
   - Stylized page, sheet, or folder shape (minimal, not literal)
   - Grid or lattice pattern (Google Sheets affinity, data structure)
   - Flowing lines suggesting data transformation

3. **Connection (optional):**
   - Subtle node/network motif
   - Or silhouettes of multiple figures (teachers, students) arranged radially
   - Or arcs/bridges connecting points

**Recommended:** Combine a central "Aldo" symbol (letter A or abstract form) with radiating emanation. Avoid clutter; keep focal point clear.

---

### Name Integration

- The word "Aldo" or letter "A" should be *embeddable* in the icon, not bolted on
- Ideally, the icon *is* Aldo — it personalizes the system as if Aldo is the active agent
- At larger scales (app icon), can include "ALDO" text lockup; at favicon size, icon-only must stand alone

---

### Tone & Emotion

- **Professional but approachable** — fits both formal school memos and casual teacher coordination
- **Energetic, not static** — suggests movement, automation, ongoing action (emanation, not a stored archive)
- **Trustworthy** — conveys reliability, order, data integrity
- **Italian sensibility** — something that doesn't feel imported; consider warm, human, slightly elegant treatment

---

### Use Cases (Size/Context)

1. **Favicon** (16×16, 32×32, 64×64): Icon-only, high contrast, readable at tiny scale
2. **App Icon** (256×256, 512×512): Can be more detailed; works in Google Drive file context
3. **UI Buttons** (24×24 up to 48×48): Quick actions, menu items, status indicators
4. **Marketing/Print** (1000×1000+): Branding, documentation covers, social media
5. **Dark/Light Theme:** Must work on both light backgrounds (school docs, Google Sheets) and dark (sidebar, dark mode UI)

---

### Technical Specifications

- **Format:** SVG (preferred for crisp scaling) or high-res PNG (512×512 minimum)
- **Dimensions:** Design at 256×256 or 512×512, ensuring 8px padding for breathing room
- **Stroke style:** Consistent line weight (typically 2–3px at 256×256)
- **Colors:** Specify in hex or RGB; include grayscale/monochrome fallback
- **Accessibility:** High contrast ratio (WCAG AA minimum 4.5:1 against white/dark backgrounds)
- **No text inside icon** unless absolutely necessary; rely on shape + color

---

### Brand Identity Hints

- Google Workspace ecosystem → clean, sans-serif influenced aesthetic (but not a copy of Google's Material Design)
- Italian school context → suggestion of heritage, warmth, human-centered design (not cold or impersonal)
- Data-driven automation → modern, tech-forward, but grounded in practical school needs

---

### Deliverables

1. **Icon** in primary color (256×512px minimum)
2. **Icon** in secondary color variant (optional)
3. **Grayscale/monochrome** version (one color, full contrast)
4. **Icon in context** — shown at favicon size (32×32) and UI size (48×48) to verify legibility
5. **Color spec sheet** — hex values, usage guidelines
6. **Brief rationale** — 2–3 sentences explaining symbolism and design choices

---

## Reference Inspiration (Optional)

- Look at how Google Workspace apps use emanation/connection metaphors (Drive, Docs, Classroom)
- Italian educational/cultural institutions' branding (elegant, not overly corporate)
- Modern icon systems that blend geometric precision with subtle humanity (not sterile)

---

## Questions to Ask the Designer

- Does the icon feel like it represents *automation* and *communication*, or just one?
- Is the emanation concept clear at small scale (favicon)?
- Would you recognize this as "school-related" without context?
- Does it work equally well in monochrome?
- Can you imagine this icon becoming the "mascot" face of ALDO in the UI?

---

## Final Note

The icon should feel like **Aldo is actively *doing* something** — not passively storing or displaying data, but creating, sending, emanating. It's the visual embodiment of the system's core identity: creator and notifier of documents.
